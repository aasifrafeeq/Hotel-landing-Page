About Munnar Valley

Munnar Valley Resort is your gateway to the tranquil beauty of the Western Ghats. Embraced by the cool, misty climate and lush tea plantations, we offer a sanctuary where nature and luxury coexist in perfect harmony.

Whether you seek the wild allure of Thekkady, the heights of Anachal, or the classic charm of Munnar, our properties promise an unforgettable experience wrapped in mist and comfort.
